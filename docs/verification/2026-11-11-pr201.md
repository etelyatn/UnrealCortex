## Limits

- The Clang probe is syntax-only on Windows. It uses no shared PCH and MSVC 14.38 headers. The other errors in four units (`FTimerHandle`, `SetFaultForTesting`, `TCondensedJsonPrintPolicy`) appear identically before and after and come from the missing PCH, not from the candidate.
- No local Linux build, link or run. The OpenSSL code was compiled and executed on Win64 against the engine's OpenSSL, not on a Unix host.
- Contributor-reported results remain attributed to the contributor: a UE 5.8.2 Linux source build with 0 errors and 0 warnings, and the listed suites passing under `-nullrhi`.
- macOS keeps the explicit unsupported-platform error in all three SHA-256 owners.
- Native runs included unrelated pre-existing uncommitted CortexAnimation edits in the workspace.
