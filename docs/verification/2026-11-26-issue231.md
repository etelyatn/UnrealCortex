Reruns on the same binaries passed 377/377 (first candidate) and 378/378 (final code).

The new review-driven tests (UndoRestoresLinks, AggregateRestrictions, NumericClamps, and the overlap, sub-precision token and oversized-readback cases) were written together with their corrections; they were not run against the uncorrected code.
